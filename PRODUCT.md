# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gym staff run a small CrossFit competition from laptops or tablets in a busy venue. They create teams and workouts, assign teams to four floor lanes, enter results quickly, and correct mistakes when needed. Spectators and athletes use a public read-only leaderboard on phones or a shared display.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable, low-friction competition desk for a small event. Staff should be able to configure the event before race day, keep heats moving during the event, and publish trustworthy standings immediately after each result is submitted.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, gritty, competitive. The public experience should feel connected to CF100 campaign artwork, while the staff tools remain calm enough for accurate work under pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic pastel SaaS dashboards, soft wellness branding, decorative fitness-app charts, excessive card grids, and interfaces that trade legibility for distressed visual effects.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the floor moving: the next useful action must always be obvious.
2. Make scoring trustworthy: preserve raw results, expose status clearly, and make corrections deliberate.
3. Separate operations from spectacle: staff screens optimize accuracy; public screens carry the event energy.
4. Show eligibility before choice: never make staff remember which teams still need a score.
5. Design for the venue: prioritize tablet use, quick scanning, and full-screen leaderboard display.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target a general small-event audience without specialized compliance requirements. Maintain sensible contrast, visible focus states, readable type, and touch-friendly controls as baseline product quality.
